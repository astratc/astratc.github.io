Why Keel

  • Readable signatures. Before you approve anything, Keel shows what it does: "This lets app X move up to 50 USDC from your wallet."
  • Spending limits by default. Unlimited token approvals are flagged and capped unless you choose otherwise.
  • Your keys never leave your device. No accounts, no email, no custody.

Features

  • Base and Ethereum mainnet
  • One-tap revoke for old approvals
  • Address book with typo protection
  • Hardware-wallet support

FAQ

Does Keel hold my funds? No. Keel is self-custody; only you hold your recovery phrase. Is it free? The app is free. Network fees go to the network, not to us. What if I lose my phone? Restore with your recovery phrase on any device.
